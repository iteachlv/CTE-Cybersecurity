Course tracks

Four courses, one clearance path

Each course builds on the last — from safe hands-on basics to certification-level networking and security practice.

CLR-01 · Foundations

Cybersecurity I

An entry-level course introducing the fundamentals of computing, safe system use, and the cybersecurity mindset.

  • Digital citizenship
  • OS basics
  • Intro security concepts
CLR-02 · Certification track

Cybersecurity II

Students go deeper into networking, operating systems, and the cybersecurity lifecycle, working toward the state end-of-program exam and industry certificate.

  • Network theory
  • Forensics
  • State EOC exam
CLR-03 · Advanced Studies

Cyber Advanced Studies

An applied, exam-focused course where students work through CompTIA CertMaster Learn + Practice toward Security+.

  • CompTIA Security+
  • CertMaster Learn
  • Practice exams
CLR-04 · Pilot

AP Networking

A college-level networking course covering protocols, infrastructure, and hands-on virtual lab environments.

  • Protocols & topology
  • VMware labs
  • College-level pacing
